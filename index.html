Georgios Antoniou

I am a theoretical physicist working on strong gravity, black holes, compact objects, and gravitational waves. My research explores how observations of black-hole dynamics can be used to test General Relativity and possible extensions of it.

I am particularly interested in black-hole perturbation theory, quasinormal modes, greybody factors, scattering problems, and effective or parametrized approaches to gravity beyond General Relativity.

My work combines analytical calculations with numerical methods for wave propagation, compact-object phenomenology, and gravitational-wave signatures of strong-field physics.
