Theoretical Physics · Strong Gravity · Gravitational Waves

Georgios Antoniou

I am a theoretical physicist working on black holes, compact objects, gravitational waves, and tests of gravity beyond General Relativity.

My research focuses on the dynamics of black-hole perturbations, quasinormal modes, greybody factors, and phenomenological frameworks for connecting strong-gravity theory with gravitational-wave observations.
